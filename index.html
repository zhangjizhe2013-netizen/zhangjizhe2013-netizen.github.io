About

Connecting photonics with precision measurement

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of Chemistry, Chinese Academy of Sciences. My current work focuses on excited-state spins in organic molecular crystals, spin-dependent optical readout, magneto-optical effects, and weak-field sensing.

During my Ph.D. at the University of Science and Technology of China, I worked on thin-film lithium-niobate photonic chips, including high-frequency acousto-optic modulation, electro-optically tunable delay, and reconfigurable on-chip photonic structures.

Research

From integrated devices to molecular spins

Three connected themes define my research trajectory.

01

Current focus

Molecular spin optical readout

Using host-guest molecular crystals to understand and enhance optical readout of photoexcited triplet spins, with magnetic-field effects and precision sensing as key observables.

  • Molecular crystals
  • Spin photophysics
  • Weak-field sensing
02

Device foundation

Integrated photonic control

Designing and characterizing thin-film lithium-niobate photonic devices for high-frequency acousto-optic modulation, electro-optic tuning, and on-chip optical delay.

  • Lithium niobate
  • Acousto-optics
  • Electro-optics
03

Precision platform

Weak-light precision measurement

Combining low-noise optical detection, modulation, synchronous acquisition, and feedback control to measure weak fluorescence and magneto-optical signals.

  • Low-noise readout
  • Magneto-optics
  • System integration
