Research Profile 科研概述

Experimental researcher working across integrated photonics, precision weak-light detection, and molecular spin quantum sensing. My doctoral research established a foundation in thin-film lithium-niobate photonic devices, high-frequency acousto-optic and electro-optic control, and reconfigurable photonic structures. My current postdoctoral work investigates excited-state spins in organic molecular crystals, spin-dependent optical readout, magneto-optical effects, and weak-field sensing.

Selected Research Experience 主要科研工作

01

Molecular excited-state spin optical readout and weak-field sensing

Study spin-dependent photophysics in organic molecular crystals, using host-guest interactions to enhance optical readout of triplet states. Develop weak-fluorescence and magnetic-field-response measurements for precision sensing and future integration with micro/nanophotonic structures.

02

Thin-film lithium-niobate photonic devices

Designed, fabricated, and characterized integrated photonic devices for 7 GHz visible acousto-optic modulation, electro-optically tunable coupling, and on-chip optical delay.

03

Optically driven micro-actuators for reconfigurable photonics

Built an optical-actuation experiment and precision-displacement measurement platform, achieving programmable approximately 30 nm steps and post-fabrication tuning of on-chip photonic structures.

Future Research Directions 未来研究方向

  • Integrated enhancement of weak optical signals: waveguides, microcavities, and high-speed modulation for efficient collection and low-noise readout.
  • On-chip readout of molecular spins: coupling organic spin systems to integrated photonic structures for compact, high-contrast magnetic sensing.
